01 — Client & Context

Mashreq Neo is the flagship digital banking product of Mashreq Bank, UAE. In 2020, the product was ready but there was no sales team, no branch support, and no prior digital acquisition infrastructure. The entire growth model had to be built from scratch, through purely digital channels with an automated KYC journey.

The initial budget was $40K. The mandate was clear: prove that a regulated banking product could be acquired at scale, end-to-end, without any human sales layer.

02 — The Challenge

The "Zero Touch" Mandate

Standard digital lead generation would not work here. A lead was worthless unless the account was funded. This meant the funnel had to handle three things simultaneously: regulatory-compliant KYC, frictionless user experience, and real-time optimisation for a conversion event that happened days after the ad click.

High Volume, Low Quality — The Default

Initial campaigns generated cheap leads but they failed KYC or never funded the account. Call centres were flooded with junk. Compliance friction meant standard pixel tracking for conversions was off the table.

03 — Strategy & Technology

The KPI was shifted entirely from Cost Per Lead to Cost Per Funded Account (CPFA). Every channel, audience, and creative decision was evaluated against funded account outcomes, not form fills.

The Offline Conversion Loop (OCAPI)

Built a server-side integration via n8n that only fired a conversion signal back to Meta and Google when the CRM status changed to "Account Funded." This trained the algorithm on quality, not volume. Started as a manual CSV upload, evolved into real-time CAPI integration.

Zero-Touch Retargeting Funnel

Built a friction-free retargeting journey for every drop-off point in the KYC flow. Automated email and SMS triggers re-engaged applicants at the exact step they abandoned. Mid-funnel drop-offs were recovered without any manual outreach.

Omni-Channel Performance (Meta + Google)

Ran parallel acquisition across Meta and Google with tightly segmented audiences. Used value-based lookalikes seeded from funded account data. Creative was modular and tested against funded account downstream conversion, not CTR.
