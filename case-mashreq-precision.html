01 - The Closed-Loop Opportunity

Mashreq Bank held 47,000 highly affluent customers already using premium credit cards or paying off high-value mortgages on luxury Emaar and Damac villas - spending upwards of AED 25,000 a month on the bank's plastic. But their primary wealth relationships sat with competitors: ENBD, FAB, and CBD.

The mandate: convert at least 1,500 of these specific individuals into fully funded Mashreq Gold members, each requiring a AED 500,000 investment or an AED 80,000 monthly salary transfer - on a hard cap of $10,000 in performance marketing spend.

02 - Piercing Through the Noise Without Wastage

The obstacle wasn't finding new audiences - it was inertia within an existing base. High-earning expats and local executives don't switch primary banking relationships for minor incentives, and a AED 2,000 cash-discount promotion meant nothing to individuals earning AED 80,000 a month or holding multi-million-dirham property portfolios.

With spend capped at $10,000, there was no room for a single dirham of waste on broad demographic or lookalike targeting. Every dollar had to hit the exact digital profiles of the existing 47,000 users - a fully closed-loop campaign.

03 - Custom Audience Synchronization & Aspirational Hooks

The $10,000 budget was used exclusively for custom audience matching on Meta, Google, and LinkedIn. By securely uploading masked contact data for the 47,000-user segment, ads appeared only on their personal social and professional feeds - reinforced by zero-cost internal channels running the same message in parallel.

Phase 1 - The Tech Concierge (Days 1–15)

An identical message ran across push notifications, dedicated HTML email, and Mashreq app banners, reinforced by custom-audience digital ads: fund a Mashreq Gold relationship and receive a custom-configured, top-tier iPhone Pro Max, delivered by private courier within 48 hours. Because paid ads matched the internal CRM list precisely, frequency was tightly optimised - driving immediate action from high-earners drawn to the status and friction-free upgrade.

Phase 2 - Closed-Loop Asset Protection (Days 16–45)

For Emaar and Damac home-loan holders within the same pool, the strategy pivoted to asset insulation using Mashreq's own wealth solutions. A corporate partnership with Sukoon Insurance provided three years of complimentary "Platinum Home Umbrella" coverage - up to AED 200,000 in home contents, AED 100,000 in personal belongings, and AED 3,000,000 in liability coverage - framed around protecting a multi-million-dirham home portfolio.

Risk Safeguards

Every reward - the tech suite and the three-year Sukoon Platinum coverage - was legally tied to a 12-month balance-maintenance clause, keeping the newly acquired assets sticky and mitigating short-term capital flight back to competitors.
