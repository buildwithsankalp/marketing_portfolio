01 — Client & Context

Etihad Airways is a global airline managing digital acquisition for direct bookings and Etihad Guest loyalty membership. The challenge is structural: airline inventory is perishable (empty seats expire), OTA commissions to Expedia and Skyscanner erode margin, and post-iOS14 signal loss broke the retargeting models that direct-booking campaigns had depended on.

Engaged as part of Zapcom's integrated marketing team for Etihad, managing the full performance marketing and loyalty acquisition portfolio.

02 — The Challenge

Signal Loss & Broken Retargeting

iOS14 wiped approximately 40% of "Add to Cart" and fare search signals. Travellers who searched for flights could no longer be reliably retargeted. The standard dynamic ad model — re-engage people who looked at a route — was broken and expensive. Attribution models were producing unreliable ROAS numbers.

OTA Competition & Perishable Inventory

Every booking through Expedia or Skyscanner costs Etihad a commission. Every empty seat on departure is revenue that disappears permanently. The two problems compound: signal loss makes efficient direct booking harder, pushing more volume to OTAs, while routes with empty seats need rapid, targeted advertising to fill them cost-effectively.

03 — Strategy & Technology

Two structural interventions: replace cookie-dependent retargeting with first-party PNR data, and restructure the budget allocation model to systematically build the loyalty data asset alongside performance.

The 20/35/45 Budget Architecture

Restructured budget allocation away from a pure performance model into a tiered funnel designed to systematically grow the first-party data asset:

20%
Brand

Destination marketing. Awareness at the route and destination level to seed the consideration funnel before purchase intent crystallises.

35%
Loyalty

Etihad Guest membership acquisition. First-party data acquisition at $8–$14 CPL. Every loyalty member becomes a cookie-free retargeting asset.

45%
Perf.

High-intent direct booking. Performance budget routed to audiences seeded by the loyalty graph — bypassing cookies entirely.

The PNR Data Loop

Integrated Sabre and NDC booking data into the measurement stack. Used the Passenger Name Record (PNR) as the global deduplication key — allowing accurate attribution across the booking funnel without relying on browser cookies. Routing: GTM Server-Side to Meta CAPI. This created a closed measurement loop that produced reliable ROAS signals despite iOS14 signal loss.

Predictive Inventory Ads

Evolution from standard dynamic ads to predictive inventory ads: bidding strategy adapted to bid higher on routes with seat availability pressure. Routes approaching departure with empty seats received elevated bid multipliers — turning perishable inventory into a bidding signal rather than a problem.
