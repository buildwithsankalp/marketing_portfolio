01 — Context & Origin

AI-powered answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE, Claude) have fundamentally changed how B2B buyers and consumers research before purchase. They no longer click ten blue links — they ask a question and receive a synthesised answer. The brand cited in that answer wins the consideration, whether or not they appear in paid search.

AEO Grader is a proprietary framework developed to score and improve content and brand assets for visibility in AI-generated answers — not just traditional search rankings. Deployed first in the Cybersecurity vertical (Radware via Zapcom), then expanded to Etihad Airways.

02 — The Challenge

Search is No Longer Just Search

Traditional SEO optimises for the top 10 blue links. AEO addresses a different question: when a CISO asks ChatGPT "what is the best DDoS protection for enterprise?" — does your brand appear in the answer? When a traveller asks Perplexity "which airline has the best direct routes from Abu Dhabi?" — does Etihad surface?

For both verticals, the answer was no. Existing content was optimised for keywords, not for AI answer synthesis. Structured data was absent or incomplete. Authority signals were weak across the sources AI models draw from.

03 — The AEO Grader Framework

The framework grades brand content across five dimensions critical to AI citation probability:

Citability

Is the content structured in a way that AI models can extract and cite as a direct answer?

Authority Signals

Are the right third-party sources — forums, review sites, publications — citing or referencing the brand?

Schema & Structure

Is structured data (FAQ, HowTo, Product schema) implemented to surface content in rich results and AI extractors?

Entity Clarity

Is the brand entity clearly defined across the web — Wikipedia, Wikidata, knowledge graphs — for AI model disambiguation?

Freshness & Breadth

Is content updated frequently enough, and does it cover the full semantic field of buyer questions?

Impression Share

Is the brand present across the distribution points where AI models pull training and retrieval signals?

Deployment: Cybersecurity

For Radware (via Zapcom), AEO Grader was used to improve visibility in AI-generated answers for enterprise security queries. Content was restructured around specific CISO questions, schema was implemented across technical documentation, and authority signals were built through G2, Reddit, and security community forums where AI models draw heavily for enterprise software responses.

Deployment: Etihad Airways

For Etihad, AEO was applied to route and destination content to improve citation rates in travel AI tools (Perplexity, ChatGPT travel queries). Structured data for routes, destinations, and loyalty programme was implemented. Loyalty programme content was restructured to surface Etihad Guest in AI answers around airline loyalty comparisons.
