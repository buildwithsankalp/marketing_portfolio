01 — Client & Context

Following the success of Mashreq Neo, the broader digital acquisition portfolio was brought in-house. Mashreq Gold is the bank's high-net-worth banking product, requiring a minimum AED 500,000 deposit. This is a fundamentally different acquisition problem — one where volume is the enemy of quality.

The mandate: own digital acquisition across the full Mashreq banking portfolio, including Gold, while maintaining compliance, protecting the brand, and driving measurable acquisition revenue.

02 — The Challenge

The Quality Problem

Initial campaigns were cheap on CPL but catastrophic on downstream quality. Call centres were flooded with leads that failed the AED 500K deposit eligibility check — generating massive opex waste and zero revenue. The algorithm was optimising for volume, not value.

Compliance Friction

Strict UAE banking regulations meant standard pixel-based conversion tracking was non-compliant. The real conversion event — a compliance-approved, funded HNW account — happened offline, days after the ad click, invisible to the ad platforms.

03 — Strategy & Technology

The entire optimisation model was rebuilt around offline conversion signals. Instead of cheap leads, the algorithm was trained on "Compliance Approved" accounts only.

Server-Side Validation (OCAPI)

Built a server-side conversion loop: the pixel only fired when CRM status changed to "Compliance Approved." Started as a manual CSV upload to Meta/Google. Evolved into a real-time CAPI integration that continuously trained the bidding algorithm on quality signals rather than form fill volume.

Value-Based Lookalikes

Stopped targeting generic demographics. Seeded lookalike audiences from existing HNW customers with AED 500K+ deposits. This narrowed reach significantly but collapsed the cost of a compliant account.

UAE's First AI-Driven Retargeting Funnel

Deployed an AI-driven retargeting model that nurtured high-value prospects over a 60-day window — significantly beyond the standard 7–30 day attribution window. Sequential messaging adapted to engagement signals across the consideration period for a major financial decision.
