01 — Client & Context

Mashreq Bank's insurance portfolio (bancassurance) had a digital marketing channel that had largely been written off. Performance was poor, costs were high, and confidence in digital acquisition for insurance products was near zero. The business relied almost entirely on branch and call centre leads.

The task was to diagnose the channel, fix the fundamentals, and prove digital could become a meaningful lead source for the insurance business.

02 — The Challenge

A Channel With No Credibility

Digital had been tried and dismissed. CPL was high, lead quality was poor, and agents weren't converting digital leads. The scepticism from product and sales teams was structural — any new approach would need to show results fast to survive internal politics.

Insurance Lead Quality vs. Volume

Generic digital campaigns for insurance in the UAE generate high volumes of leads with very low intent. Cheap leads that never progress to policy sale are not just useless — they create operational cost in call centre handling and erode confidence in the channel further.

03 — Strategy & Technology

Instead of chasing cheap leads, the strategy focused on isolating "interested leads" — prospects who engaged meaningfully with insurance content before converting. The funnel was rebuilt around intent signals rather than form fills.

Audience Precision & Segmentation

Rebuilt audience targeting around life-stage signals: expatriates with dependants, professionals with employer insurance gaps, homeowners with protection needs. Replaced generic demographic targeting with contextual and behavioural segments aligned to actual insurance purchase triggers.

Creative Strategy: Simplifying Actuarial Complexity

Insurance messaging in MENA defaults to fear and compliance. Rebuilt creative to make insurance journeys simple and agile — translating complex actuarial data into single-insight ads that made agents nod and customers act. Tested benefit framing, protection storytelling, and digital journey clarity.

Digital Journey Optimisation

Worked with the product team on user testing and market research to identify friction points in the digital insurance journey. Simplified the digital application flow, improving conversion from click to submitted application.
